04 / MECHANICAL DESIGN

Mechanical Design Work.

A growing collection of CAD, mechanical assemblies, and design explorations focused on translating functional requirements into manufacturable components.

SOLIDWORKS3D CADDESIGN FOR FABRICATION

Design philosophy

Good mechanical design balances function, manufacturability, assembly, cost, and reliability. My design work emphasizes understanding the purpose of each component, choosing an appropriate geometry, and considering how individual parts interact within an assembly.

Typical workflow

  • Identify the intended function and primary design constraints.
  • Develop component geometry using parametric 3D CAD.
  • Build assemblies and check fit, motion, and interference.
  • Consider material selection, fabrication methods, tolerances, and fastening.
  • Revise the design based on analysis, prototype feedback, and practical constraints.

Areas of interest

Mechanical systems, lightweight structures, brackets and fixtures, mechanisms, prototyping, and design approaches relevant to aerospace hardware.

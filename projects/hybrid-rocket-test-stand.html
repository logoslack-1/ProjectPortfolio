03 / PROPULSION TEST INFRASTRUCTURE

Hybrid Rocket Test Stand.

Designed and structurally validated a modular test stand intended to support multiple hybrid rocket engine configurations and measure thrust through a load cell.

SOLIDWORKSFINITE ELEMENT ANALYSISSTRUCTURAL REDESIGNLOAD-CELL INTEGRATION

Project objective

The test stand was modeled as a modular structure to accommodate more than one engine configuration while providing a load path for measuring thrust with a load cell. Structural integrity under the maximum design load was a primary consideration before fabrication.

DESIGN REQUIREMENT

Accommodate multiple engine configurations and transmit the resulting thrust load through the stand to a load cell while maintaining structural compliance for planned hot-fire operations.

Initial structural analysis

Finite Element Analysis was performed on the initial stand design. The first analysis identified a critical failure point under maximum load, showing that the original load-bearing geometry required revision before the structure could proceed.

Redesign and improved FEA

The load-bearing members were re-engineered with expanded cross-sections to address the stress concentrations identified in the initial iteration. The updated geometry was then checked with a second FEA iteration. The project portfolio documents the improved model as structurally compliant for sustained hot-fire operations prior to fabrication.

ITERATION 01 / INITIAL DESIGN

Initial frame configuration — FEA identified a critical location under maximum load.

ITERATION 02 / REVISED DESIGN

Revised load-bearing geometry — larger cross-sections were assessed in the improved FEA iteration.

Engineering workflow

  • Develop a modular CAD model sized around the intended engine and load-cell arrangement.
  • Apply the relevant structural loading in the initial FEA model.
  • Review the stress / failure indication and identify the critical region under maximum load.
  • Increase the cross-sections of the load-bearing members to reduce the identified stress concentrations.
  • Re-run the structural analysis and assess the revised configuration against the design requirements.

Outcome and scope

The documented outcome is a revised test-stand design that met the stated structural requirement in the improved FEA model before fabrication. This is a simulation and design-validation result; the project material supplied here does not report a completed fabrication or physical hot-fire test.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

The analysis informed a concrete design change: identify the critical load path, revise the member geometry, and use a follow-up FEA iteration to evaluate the improved structure.
